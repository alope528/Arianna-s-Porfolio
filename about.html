About Me

Hi, I'm Arianna! I'm a Media Arts and Sciences student at ASU with an interest in web development, digital design, and interactive media. I enjoy exploring the intersection of technology and creativity, and I'm always looking for ways to combine creativity with technology to create digital experiences.

My Interests

Some of my interests include web design, graphic design, video games, digital art, and interactive media experiences. I'm passionate about creating engaging digital experiences that blend aesthetics with functionality. I especially enjoy projects that allow me to experiment with both visual design and technology.

My Goals

As I continue my education, I aim to strengthen my skills in web development, programming, digital design, and learn more about the field of digital media. My goal is to build a variety of projects that demonstrate both my creativity and technical abilities while continuing to develop my portfolio. I aspire to create innovative and interactive digital experiences that leave a lasting impact on users.
